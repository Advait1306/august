We're excited to introduce August, a new way for teams to work with AI agents.

## Why we built August

Teams today face a unique challenge: AI is powerful, but it's fragmented. Everyone uses different tools, different prompts, and different workflows. Knowledge stays siloed. Your junior developers don't benefit from what your senior engineers have learned about working with AI.

August changes that.

## How it works

August lets you create, manage, and deploy AI agents across your entire team. When one person discovers a better way to work with AI, everyone benefits.

### Shared skills

Skills are reusable prompts and workflows that anyone on your team can use. When your best engineer figures out how to get great code reviews from AI, they can share that skill with the whole team.

### Team memory

Your agents learn from your codebase, your documentation, and your team's collective knowledge. The more you use August, the more helpful it becomes.

### Connect to your tools

August integrates with the applications you already use. Your agents work where you work.

## What's next

We're just getting started. Over the coming months, we'll be adding:

- More integrations with popular development tools
- Advanced workflow automation
- Team analytics and insights
